## 📌 Project Overview

**Nassau Candy Data Analysis** is an end-to-end data analytics and business intelligence project developed to transform raw distributor data into meaningful business insights.

The project analyzes **sales, costs, gross profit, profit margins, products, divisions, factories, and time-based trends** to identify business performance patterns and areas for improvement.

The analysis is presented through an interactive **Streamlit dashboard** containing KPIs, visualizations, analytical tables, filters, business insights, and recommendations.

The project demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Analysis → Business Analysis → Visualization → Insights → Recommendations → Interactive Dashboard**

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Understand the structure and quality of the raw dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify the most profitable products.
- Compare business divisions.
- Analyze monthly and yearly performance trends.
- Identify products contributing the majority of total profit.
- Analyze margin and profitability risks.
- Evaluate factory-level performance.
- Generate actionable business insights.
- Build an interactive dashboard for decision-making.

---

# 💼 Business Questions

This project attempts to answer important business questions such as:

### Sales Performance
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which periods generate the highest sales?
- Which products contribute most to total sales?

### Profitability
- Which products generate the highest gross profit?
- Which products have strong or weak profit margins?
- Which divisions are most profitable?

### Product Performance
- Which products are high-value contributors?
- Which products have low profitability?
- What percentage of products contributes most of the total profit?

### Division Performance
- Which divisions generate the highest revenue?
- Which divisions generate the highest gross profit?
- How do margins differ between divisions?

### Factory Performance
- Which factories contribute the most sales?
- Which factories generate the highest gross profit?
- Which factories/products require additional attention?

### Business Risk
- Which products have low margins?
- Which products combine significant sales with weak margins?
- Where should management focus improvement efforts?

# 📊 Key Analyses

## 1. 🔍 Data Understanding

The first stage focuses on understanding the raw dataset.

Activities include:

- Dataset structure inspection
- Column identification
- Data type inspection
- Missing-value analysis
- Duplicate checking
- Descriptive statistics
- Data quality assessment

---

## 2. 🧹 Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis is performed to understand patterns and distributions within the dataset.

The analysis examines:

- Sales distribution
- Cost distribution
- Profit distribution
- Product-level performance
- Division-level performance
- Time-based patterns
- Relationships between sales, cost, and profit

---

## 3. 💰 Product Profitability Analysis

Product profitability is analyzed using key financial metrics such as:

- Sales
- Cost
- Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin

This analysis helps identify:

- High-profit products
- Low-profit products
- High-margin products
- Low-margin products
- Products requiring business attention

---

## 4. 🏢 Division Analysis

The project evaluates business performance across different divisions.

The analysis compares:

- Total Sales
- Total Cost
- Gross Profit
- Profit Margin
- Contribution to overall business performance

This helps determine which divisions are stronger contributors to profitability.

---

## 5. 📅 Monthly & Yearly Trend Analysis

Time-series analysis is performed to understand business performance over time.

The project analyzes:

- Monthly sales trends
- Monthly profit trends
- Monthly margin trends
- Monthly unit trends
- Yearly sales performance
- Yearly profitability

These trends help identify growth patterns, declines, and changes in business performance.

---

## 6. 📈 Trend Visualization

Interactive and analytical visualizations are used to make time-based patterns easier to understand.

Examples include:

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Monthly Profit Trend
- Monthly Margin Trend
- Monthly Units Trend
- Yearly Performance

---

## 7. 🎯 Pareto Profitability Analysis

Pareto analysis is used to identify the products responsible for a significant portion of total profit.

The analysis helps answer:

> Which products contribute most significantly to overall profitability?

This allows management to focus attention on high-value products rather than treating every product equally.

---

## 8. ⚠️ Margin & Risk Analysis

Margin and risk analysis identifies products based on their profitability characteristics.

The analysis helps identify:

- High-margin products
- Low-margin products
- Strong sales but weak margins
- Products requiring improvement
- Potential profitability risks

This provides a more practical business perspective than analyzing sales alone.

---

## 9. 🏭 Factory Performance Analysis

Factory-level analysis evaluates the performance of manufacturing locations.

The analysis includes:

- Factory Sales
- Factory Gross Profit
- Factory Profitability
- Product-to-Factory mapping

This helps identify stronger and weaker factory contributions.

---

## 10. 💡 Business Insights & Recommendations

The project converts analytical results into business-oriented recommendations.

Recommendations are generated based on:

- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Profit margins
- Product contribution
- Division performance
- Factory performance
- Risk indicators

The goal is to move beyond simply displaying charts and provide insights that can support business decisions.
